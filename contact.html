FAQs

Everything you need to know.

We keep the essentials clear and practical for beginner and experienced gardeners alike.

Is cocopeat enough for all plants?

No. Cocopeat alone does not provide all the nutrients plants need. Add suitable compost or fertilizer according to the plant's requirements.

Does cocopeat expand after adding water?

Yes. Cocopeat absorbs water and expands in volume. Expansion can vary depending on the amount of water and the product used.

Is this beginner-friendly?

Yes. The starter kit is designed to be easy to use for home gardeners, balcony planters and terrace growers starting out.

Can I use it for balconies and terraces?

Yes. The compact format is suited to small gardening spaces such as balconies, terraces and indoor plant corners.

Do you offer future tablet products?

We plan to launch compressed cocopeat tablets in the future. This upcoming product is currently marked as coming soon.
