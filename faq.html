CocoMitra was created for Indian home gardeners, balcony growers, plant lovers and small garden businesses looking for practical and sustainable growing essentials.

Our first offering focuses on cocopeat and simple starter kits that make everyday gardening more accessible. We are building a brand that combines thoughtful design, practical guidance and clear information for beginners and growing enthusiasts.

What we believe

  • Growing should feel simple, affordable and encouraging.
  • Quality basics matter more than complicated systems.
  • Transparent guidance helps gardeners make better decisions.

Our future direction

We plan to expand into compressed cocopeat tablets and additional sustainable gardening products that are compact, user-friendly and ready for modern home gardening lifestyles.
