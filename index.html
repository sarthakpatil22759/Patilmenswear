Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know.

Is cocopeat enough for all plants?

No. Cocopeat alone does not provide all the nutrients plants need. Add suitable compost or fertilizer based on the plant's requirements.

Does it expand when water is added?

Yes. Cocopeat expands as it absorbs water. Expansion can vary depending on the product and water quantity used.

Is this suitable for balcony gardens?

Yes. The compact nature of this starter combo makes it useful for small balcony, terrace, or home gardening setups.
